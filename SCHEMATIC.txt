TI-99/4A Keyboard Adapter — Wiring Schematic
=============================================

  ESP32-S3 N16R8 dev board (Lonely Binary Gold Edition).
  Physical pin numbers count from pin 1 (3V3) at top of left header
  down to pin 22 (GND) at the bottom. Silkscreen labels in brackets.

                            |

                     ESP32 pin 1 [3V3] +----------+----------+----------+

  Signal Flow Summary
  ====================

  Row outputs (ESP32 drives LOW to simulate key press):

      ESP32 ──► TXS A-side ──► TXS B-side ──► TI INT line
      (3.3V)    (level shift)   (5V)           (to TMS9901)

      Pin 4  [4]  GPIO 4  ──►  TI Pin 1   INT5
      Pin 5  [5]  GPIO 5  ──►  TI Pin 2   INT6
      Pin 6  [6]  GPIO 6  ──►  TI Pin 3   INT8
      Pin 7  [7]  GPIO 7  ──►  TI Pin 4   INT4
      Pin 8  [15] GPIO 15 ──►  TI Pin 5   INT3
      Pin 9  [16] GPIO 16 ──►  TI Pin 7   INT7
      Pin 15 [9]  GPIO 9  ──►  TI Pin 10  INT9
      Pin 16 [10] GPIO 10 ──►  TI Pin 11  INT10


  Column inputs (TI's 74LS156 drives LOW to select column):

      TI 74LS156 ──► TXS B-side ──► TXS A-side ──► ESP32
      (5V)           (level shift)   (3.3V)         (digitalRead)

      TI Pin 8   1Y1  ──►  GPIO 17  [17]  Pin 10  (col 0)
      TI Pin 9   1Y0  ──►  GPIO 18  [18]  Pin 11  (col 4)
      TI Pin 12  2Y0  ──►  GPIO 11  [11]  Pin 17  (col 5)
      TI Pin 13  2Y1  ──►  GPIO 12  [12]  Pin 18  (col 1)
      TI Pin 14  2Y2  ──►  GPIO 13  [13]  Pin 19  (col 2)
      TI Pin 15  2Y3  ──►  GPIO 14  [14]  Pin 20  (col 3)


  Notes
  =====

  - TI Pin 6 (P5 / Alpha Lock) is NOT connected. Alpha Lock is
    implemented in software to avoid the TI's joystick-UP bug.

  - TXS#2 channels A2/B2 and A1/B1 are not connected on either side.

  - GPIO 8 (pin 12), GPIO 3 (pin 13), and GPIO 46 (pin 14) sit in the
    physical gap between the two TXS boards and are all unused.

  - Each TXS0108E OE pin must be jumpered to ESP32 pin 1 [3V3]
    to enable the buffer. Do NOT connect OE to pin 3 [RST].

  - The TXS0108E auto-detects signal direction — no DIR pin.
